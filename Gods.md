## Ollo
God of creation. Lawful Good. Not much is known about Ollo, but is stated as being to all powerful creator of Olumal, bringer of life, protector of all. The most common god to worship in Olumal, many large churches dedicated to them. They say they can grant miracles and blessings to devout followers. 

## Quallo 
God of death. Lawful Evil. Originally a follower of Ollo, Quallo forged their own path trying to conquer the world tens of thousands of years ago. It is said he was slain and banished to the void by Ballo, and now they inhabit this void, where tormented and evil souls go to be consumed by his essence. There was a time when many followed him, armies were raised in his name, but ultimately vanquished. It is sacrilege to discuss his deeds, and blasphemy to worship him. 

## Yasso 
God of Trickery and mischief. Neutral Good. A musician and artist, performer, and story teller. Yasso was charismatic, bubbly, and a clown. He was a devout follower of Ollo, and upon his death, ascended to godhood. Known to bring joy, laughter, and the occasional mishaps upon his followers, Yasso is held in high regard. He was present during the Dark Hour, and fought alongside Olumals heroes to destroy Zello. 

## Zello 
God of Torment. Chaotic Evil. Queen of Sickness. Tyrant of Torture. Mistress of Chaos. She was a royal war chief, hell bent on seeing Quallo's enemies defeated. She raised a massive army of death thousands of years ago that scoured the land and mercilessly killed all in their path. She was slain during that war, and it is said that she ascended to a domain of hatred. A plane of existence for murders, thieves, and other nefarious folk. There you are killed and risen over and over, until at last Quallo devours your soul. 

## Ballo 
Goddess of Truth. Chaotic Good. The wind itself, rain falling, the creaking of tree branches, this is Ballos domain
